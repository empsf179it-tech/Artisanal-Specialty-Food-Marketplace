Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I transition my pet to a new artisanal food?

We recommend a slow transition over 7-10 days. Start by mixing 25% of the new food with 75% of their current food, gradually increasing the new food while decreasing the old.

Are your ingredients human-grade?

Yes, we source only the highest quality, human-grade ingredients from trusted local farmers and sustainable fisheries. We believe your companions deserve the same quality of food as you do.

Do you offer customized nutrition plans?

Absolutely! You can reach out to us using the contact form above, and our lead canine/feline nutritionists will help curate a diet tailored exactly to your pet's age, breed, and health requirements.

How should I store freeze-dried or raw food?

Freeze-dried food should be kept in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. If you are using our frozen raw selections, keep them in the freezer and thaw individual portions in the refrigerator 24 hours before feeding.

What if my pet is a picky eater?

We suggest starting with our functional bone broths or freeze-dried meal toppers. Adding a bit of warm moisture often brings out the natural aromas and entices even the most discerning pets.
